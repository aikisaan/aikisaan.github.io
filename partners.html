About aiKisaan

aiKisaan is the professional network for farmers — connecting every farmer and FPO to knowledge, community, markets, finance, and opportunity.

Our mission

India's digital social and professional network for farmers

aiKisaan transforms farming from an isolated occupation into a connected, knowledge-rich, collaborative, and economically empowered community. We believe every farmer deserves the same professional network, market intelligence, and financial tools available to any modern professional.

We are building the professional network, marketplace, intelligence layer, and community backbone that every one of India's 150 million farmers deserves — and the world's food system urgently needs.

Reduce cost

Increase revenue

Improve productivity

Improve certainty

AI Agriculture Advisor — Saathi

Voice-first, multilingual AI farming companion. Expert-quality crop advice, disease diagnosis, mandi prices, and scheme eligibility — in your language, 24/7.

Meet Saathi

Expert farming intelligence in your pocket

Saathi is accessible through voice or text in Hindi and 11 regional languages. No internet connection needed for basic advisory.

📸

Disease diagnosis

Upload a photo of your crop. Saathi identifies pests, diseases, and nutrient deficiencies — and recommends specific treatments.

₹

Mandi price intelligence

Real-time price comparison across 12+ mandis. Know whether to sell today, store, or connect with a direct buyer.

🌱

Soil and nutrient advice

Soil profile + crop requirement = a precise, field-specific nutrient plan. Avoid over-application and reduce input spend.

📋

Scheme eligibility

PM-KISAN, NHM, soil health card, crop insurance — Saathi matches your profile to every scheme you qualify for.

Key capabilities from the feature benefit map

What Saathi can do for you

Photo-based disease detection

AI-powered image recognition identifies crop diseases, pest damage, and nutrient deficiencies from a photograph.

FARMER BENEFIT
Expert-quality advice without an agronomist visit; early pest alerts prevent blanket spray costs and crop loss.

Real-time mandi price comparison

Live price data from 12+ mandis, FPO buyers, processors, and direct buyers — compared in one view.

FARMER BENEFIT

Government scheme matching

Your verified farmer profile is matched against all active central and state government agricultural schemes.

FARMER BENEFIT

FPO risk intelligence

FARMER BENEFIT

Frequently asked questions

How do I register as a farmer?▼
Click 'Join' in the navigation or visit /register and fill in the farmer form. Our team reviews every application and emails you once it has been approved. The form takes about 5 minutes.
How does the FarmERP integration work?▼
aiKisaan connects to FarmERP via REST API and webhooks. Farmer data, crop plans, lot records, and financial entries sync automatically between both systems.
Is Saathi AI available offline?▼
Basic advisory features work with limited connectivity. Full disease diagnosis and real-time mandi prices require a data connection. The app stores recent content for offline access.
How do FPOs register on aiKisaan?▼
FPOs can register at /register or contact us directly. We work through a pilot onboarding process with your FPO manager to set up member profiles and the digital cockpit.
Which languages does aiKisaan support?▼
Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Kannada, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Malayalam, Assamese, and English. Voice input and voice response are available in all 12 languages.
